# Reference research — 7 September 2026

## Scope and limits
Public reference discovery on Pinterest, Dribbble and Mobbin informed the three concepts. This is qualitative design research, not measured conversion evidence or a verified ranking of current trends. The full Mobbin library requires authenticated access and was not available. Pinterest's public indexed references were available; direct page retrieval was intermittent. Firecrawl search succeeded after using its installed runtime directly; page scraping was less reliable. No authenticated content was accessed.

## References and application
- [Dribbble gift-card storefront examples](https://dribbble.com/search/giftcard-website): tangible gift-card imagery and visible marketplace categorization. Public listings include WaterMelonMarkets and gift-card store concepts. Applied as recognizable brand imagery and immediate product access rather than copying crypto/fintech styling.
- [Dribbble gifting journey study](https://dribbble.com/shots/26234132-Gift-Marketplace-App-Design-Enhancing-the-Gifting-Experience): product choice and purchasing-flow emphasis. Applied as distinct variants and clear checkout paths; no claim that this concept has measured conversion gains.
- [Pinterest game-store design reference](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/695946948650640718/) and [game website board](https://www.pinterest.com/dreamcodesign/video-game-website-designs/): image-led gaming storefront references. Applied as a recognizable cover wall in concept 3.
- [Pinterest colorful digital-store reference](https://es.pinterest.com/pin/shopify-theme-template-colorful-shopify-website-design-pastel-shopify-theme-for-digital-kids-products-responsive-shopify-20-10vs-etsy--1147503180051140328/): color-block identity. Applied as committed cobalt/acid art direction in concept 2, without copying a template.
- [Mobbin public design examples](https://mobbin.com/mcp): its public paywall example discusses tangible product representation and price near the action. Applied as product-first heroes and clear totals. This does not imply inspection of private screens.
- [Mobbin's own merchandise storefront](https://shopify.mobbin.com/collections/frontpage): clear product names, prices, empty-cart and checkout states. Applied as uncomplicated commerce controls.

## Payment accuracy
[Chargily Pay documentation](https://dev.chargily.com/pay-v2/api-reference/introduction) and [official Chargily business page](https://chargily.com/dz/business/pay) describe CIB and Edahabia support. The prototypes therefore keep CCP/BaridiMob manual transfers separate from Chargily card checkout. Integration requirements and fulfillment policies remain future implementation work.

## Motion
[Motion JavaScript documentation](https://motion.dev/docs/quick-start) supports framework-independent DOM animation; this suits the requested HTML/CSS/JS stage without adding React solely for Framer Motion. Motion and Three.js are bundled locally. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Images and fonts
The six requested product brands use real downloaded web images. Steam-hosted game covers form the cinematic wall. assets/sources.json lists individual download URLs; ExitLag imagery is from its official site. Fonts are Google Fonts: DM Sans, Outfit, Bebas Neue and Instrument Serif, self-hosted.
