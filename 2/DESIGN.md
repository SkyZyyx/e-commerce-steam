# Kif Club — direction 02

A cultural poster shop for digital entertainment. Provisional French name and content, for Algerian customers. Cobalt (#153cda), pale blue (#e2ebfb), acid yellow (#ddf852), navy (#112460), a coral Netflix ticket. No gradients. Local Bebas Neue display type and DM Sans body; monumental condensed hero is an intentional pinned-brief exception to the generic display size floor.

Composition: gigantic two-line headline, tilted branded product tickets, a restrained editorial catalog, acid-yellow payment spread and oversize typographic footer. The poster tickets are the authored moment: clipped headline reveal and individually delayed image arrival. No perpetual decorative motion. Reduced-motion disables the entrance. Content is present before optional animation loads.

All six product types have illustrative variants and prices, category filters and search, variant-dependent price, cart add/remove, accessible native dialog, selectable demo checkout, FAQ details and compact mobile navigation. Payment card checkout names Chargily CIB/Edahabia. CCP/BaridiMob has a separate manual-transfer workflow. No purchase, credential collection or provider connection exists. No sales/reviews/availability/delivery guarantees fabricated.

Responsive two-column mobile catalog; the mobile hero preserves Steam and Spotify tickets, Netflix remains available in the visible catalog. Body scroll locks during cart use; Escape restores it; native dialog focus containment and explicit labelled controls. All product assets remain shared ../assets files.

QA: category buttons, search field, product variant select, Ajouter button, Panier, Retirer, Choisir mon paiement, both payment radios, demo confirmation button, FAQ summaries, mobile Menu. Refresh starts a fresh demonstration cart intentionally.
