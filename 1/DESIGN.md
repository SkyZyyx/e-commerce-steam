# Kif Boutique — direction 1

A warm digital gift boutique. Cream #f6f3eb, vermilion #d63d1c and olive ink #252820. DM Sans gives the large conversational typography rounded warmth without losing legibility. The first impression is an asymmetric typographic composition opposite tangible branded cards in a real Three.js scene. Restrained physical motion and a quiet plinth surface make the digital products feel collectible.

The spacious entrance settles into a tightly edited three-column collection. Six products, actual local reference imagery, French descriptions, inline variant selectors and clear DZD prices. Pill controls are limited to category filters and small tags. Product images supply color; no gradients, fabricated social proof, service promises or technical aesthetic.

Functional scope: category filters, live name search and empty state, variant pricing, additions and removal from the accessible modal cart, separate demo Chargily and manual transfer payment journeys, expandable answers, mobile menu. Price and variant information is explicitly illustrative. No purchase is created.

Motion: local Motion orchestrates the visible hero entrance; real Three.js animates three textured product boxes. Pointer response, pause, reduced-motion and image fallback. Images remain available if WebGL fails. Native dialog provides protected cart focus and Escape dismissal. Responsive two-column products and stacked hero on mobile.

Root owns the one batched desktop/mobile browser inspection and bounded correction round.
